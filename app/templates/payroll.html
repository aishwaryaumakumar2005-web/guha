    How Commission is Calculated

    For each student a tutor teaches, commission = (fees paid by that student in the period ÷ number of tutors teaching that student) × commission %. The total is added to base + bonus, then TDS and other deductions are applied.

    • Only cash actually collected (FeeRecord.amount_paid) counts — concessions/waivers are not cash and never generate commission.
    • Fees are attributed to the month they were paid in; a large advance payment lands entirely in its payment month (no pro-rating).
    • When several tutors teach one student, the student's fees are split evenly between all of them.
    • The per-student breakdown is a snapshot frozen at processing time — recalculating a draft or saving settings refreshes it.
